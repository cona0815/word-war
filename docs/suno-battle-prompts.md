# Word War｜六首 Suno 配樂描述詞

六首都以純器樂為目標，不填歌詞。下列英文段落可各自直接貼入描述欄。一般戰鬥輕快、魔法感明亮；小魔王有張力但不恐怖。建議生成後各選約 60～90 秒最穩定的段落，做遊戲循環；提示中的無縫循環是創作方向，實際循環點仍需剪輯確認。

## 1. 一般戰鬥｜校園初戰（第 1～3 關）

**氣氛：** 冒險剛開始，容易親近、讓學生有勇氣。**速度：** 108 BPM。

```text
Instrumental medieval fantasy RPG battle music, 108 BPM, bright D major with a gentle Dorian folk color. A brave young wizard and a young witch defend their magical school together. Playful lute ostinato, warm recorder melody, light pizzicato strings, soft frame drums, delicate harp arpeggios and sparkling celesta spell accents. A memorable short heroic theme, clear steady pulse for a typing game, energetic but relaxed, friendly and adventurous for elementary school children. Begin with the groove immediately, introduce the melody after four bars, add a light string response, then return to the opening groove. Consistent moderate energy, loop-friendly ending, no dramatic final stop. Clean spacious mix with room for keyboard sounds. No vocals, no chanting, no heavy bass, no horror, no EDM, no electric guitars.
```

## 2. 一般戰鬥｜雙星魔法巡邏（第 4～6 關）

**氣氛：** 男女主角合作、法術接力，節奏更有推進感。**速度：** 120 BPM。

```text
Instrumental medieval magical academy action music, 120 BPM, uplifting G Dorian and luminous major harmonies. Cooperative wizard duo fighting cute enchanted creatures in a sunny school courtyard. Interlocking lute and harp patterns represent two heroes working together; nimble woodwinds answer quick violin motifs. Rhythmic frame drums, restrained orchestral percussion, warm French horns, crystal chimes and shimmering magical accents. Start directly with a clear battle rhythm, develop two alternating short melodies, build gently with strings, return to the main groove for looping. Brave, playful, focused and optimistic, moderate intensity suitable for a children's educational typing RPG. No long intro, no sudden silence, no explosive volume spikes. Leave space for typing and spell effects. Pure instrumental, no vocals or choir, no horror, no EDM drops.
```

## 3. 一般戰鬥｜八景守護衝刺（第 7～8 關）

**氣氛：** 多隻敵人靠近，速度加快但不焦躁。**速度：** 132 BPM。

```text
Instrumental medieval fantasy RPG battle loop, 132 BPM, triumphant A minor moving toward C major. Two young academy mages protect their campus against several waves of small magical monsters. Fast but clear string ostinatos, rhythmic lute, agile flute runs, confident horn calls, tight frame drums and moderate timpani. Add small celesta and glockenspiel flashes for successful magic attacks. Immediate rhythmic start, bold four-bar heroic motif, a contrasting lighter woodwind section, then a fuller return of the same theme. Exciting and motivating without feeling frantic or threatening. Steady tempo and controlled dynamics for concentration during a typing game. Loop-friendly continuation ending, no final victory cadence. No singing, no choir, no dark horror textures, no distorted guitars, no electronic dance beat.
```

## 4. 小魔王戰｜寶石守衛甦醒（前段 Boss）

**氣氛：** 可愛小魔王登場，稍有壓力但打得贏。**速度：** 116 BPM。

```text
Instrumental medieval fantasy mini-boss battle music, 116 BPM, heroic E Dorian. A giant friendly-looking enchanted guardian awakens in a magical school garden. A short two-beat timpani entrance immediately leads into a steady battle groove. Low strings create anticipation, playful bassoon gives the monster personality, bright horns and recorder carry the young heroes' brave response. Lute, harp, frame drums and sparkling crystal spell chimes. Three connected sections: the guardian approaches, the two student mages cooperate, the heroic melody grows stronger. Keep all sections playable as continuous battle music, not a victory jingle. Tension with warmth and humor, never frightening, suitable for children. Balanced percussion, clear melody, loopable tail. Pure instrumental, no voices, no sinister choir, no jump scares or huge cinematic impacts.
```

## 5. 小魔王戰｜符文反擊（中段 Boss）

**氣氛：** 三階段攻防、閃避後反擊，魔法感更強。**速度：** 128 BPM。

```text
Instrumental medieval spellcaster mini-boss battle, 128 BPM, B minor with hopeful D major resolutions. Two young magical academy heroes face an armored rune beast. Driving staccato strings, plucked lute rhythms, deep but controlled timpani, frame drums, noble French horns and quick woodwind countermelodies. Celesta clusters and sweeping harp glissandos suggest blue and gold spells colliding. Use a repeating rhythmic motif for focus: opening duel, slightly denser counterattack, brighter determined third phase. Increase musical layers rather than loudness. Exciting, clever and courageous, never grim or violent. Short intro, no slow ambient breakdown, consistent pulse, a clean loop-friendly ending without a final hit. Keep the middle frequencies clear for game feedback. No vocals, no choir, no modern synth bass, no metal guitars, no horror atmosphere.
```

## 6. 小魔王戰｜八景寶石決戰（後段 Boss／大橋堂可用）

**氣氛：** 盛大、帥氣，象徵一路練習後的成果。**速度：** 138 BPM。

```text
Instrumental grand medieval fantasy RPG boss battle, 138 BPM, heroic D minor resolving repeatedly into radiant F major. A boy wizard and a girl wizard unite their magic beneath eight glowing gems in a magnificent academy hall. Full but transparent orchestral arrangement: energetic strings, bold French horns and trumpets, medieval lute and recorder, rolling timpani, disciplined orchestral drums, harp and brilliant celesta magic sparkles. A strong memorable heroic theme shared between brass and woodwinds. Begin with a very short royal fanfare, move immediately into the battle pulse, develop a tense magical duel, then return with a powerful hopeful statement of the theme. Epic achievement and teamwork, suitable for elementary school heroes, inspiring rather than frightening. Keep volume stable and leave room for keyboard and spell sounds. End on a rhythmic turnaround for an editable loop, not a completed victory ending. No vocals, no choir, no horror, no distorted bass, no EDM drops.
```

## 六首共同的調整方向

- 若太像電影預告片：加上 `continuous gameplay music, stable dynamics, no trailer impacts, no long cinematic buildup`。
- 若魔法感不足：加上 `more celesta, harp, crystal chimes and playful woodwind spell motifs`。
- 若搶走打字注意力：加上 `simpler melody, softer percussion, less dense arrangement, clear steady pulse`。
- 輸出檔名建議：`battle-campus-01`、`battle-duo-02`、`battle-gems-03`、`boss-guardian-01`、`boss-runes-02`、`boss-hall-03`。
